chatbrief

Turn old AI chats into a short context pack you can paste into any AI to carry on where you left off. Everything happens in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Pick the export files you downloaded from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok or DeepSeek (.zip or .json).

2. Tick the chats that belong to the project

3. Your context pack

You can edit the pack before copying it. Paste it as the first message of a new chat.

How it picks what goes in

  • It does not use an AI. It reads your own messages and keeps sentences that look like preferences ("I want", "always", "don't"), decisions ("let's go with", "I chose") and things still to do, plus how each chat started and where it left off.
  • That makes it private and free, but it works by spotting phrases, so it can miss things or keep the wrong sentence. Read the pack once before you rely on it.
  • The phrase rules are English. Chats in other languages still get the start and the "left off" parts.

Part of the same family as chatvault, chatzip and chatrelay.
